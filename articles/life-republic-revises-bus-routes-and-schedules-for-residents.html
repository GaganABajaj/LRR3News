Life Republic Revises Bus Routes and Schedules for Residents

The Life Republic Facility Management Team announced a revised schedule for bus services, following suggestions received from residents. The revision follows the earlier circular dated 16 August 2026 and aims to make commuting more convenient for residents.

Revised Bus Schedule and Route Timings

Residents can refer to the revised schedule below for bus timings and destinations:

  • Blue Line: Life Republic ↔ Wipro Circle
  • Pink Line: Life Republic ↔ Embassy Tech Zone
  • Yellow Line: Life Republic ↔ Phoenix Millennium

Key Departure Timings

Blue Line: Life Republic ↔ Wipro Circle Direction Departure Timings From Life Republic 8:00 AM, 9:00 AM, 6:30 PM From Wipro Circle (Return) 8:30 AM, 6:00 PM, 7:00 PM Pink Line: Life Republic ↔ Embassy Tech Zone Direction Departure Timings From Life Republic 8:30 AM, 9:30 AM, 7:00 PM From Embassy Tech Zone (Return) 9:00 AM, 6:30 PM, 7:30 PM Yellow Line: Life Republic ↔ Phoenix Millennium Direction Departure Timings From Life Republic 8:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 5:00 PM, 7:00 PM From Phoenix Millennium (Return) 9:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 4:00 PM, 6:00 PM, 8:00 PM

Residents are encouraged to review the attached revised route map and detailed bus schedule for the complete list of stops and trip timings and to plan their journeys accordingly.

Important Instructions for Passengers

1. Boarding and Timings
  • Please arrive at your stop 5 minutes early. The bus will wait a maximum of 5 minutes at stops.
  • Please queue while boarding and allow passengers to exit first.
  • Timings may vary due to traffic, road conditions, weather, or public events. Please cooperate in case of delays.
2. Passenger Safety and Courtesy
  • Priority seats are reserved for senior citizens, pregnant women, and passengers with disabilities. Please vacate them when required.
  • Keep aisles and emergency exits clear at all times.
  • Do not tamper with emergency equipment, including fire extinguishers, hammers, or first-aid kits.
  • Children must be accompanied by an adult and should not be left unattended.
  • Treat fellow passengers and staff with courtesy and respect.
3. Prohibited Activities and Cleanliness
  • Smoking, alcohol, and tobacco products are prohibited on the bus and at stops.
  • Pets are not permitted.
  • Avoid eating inside the bus. If necessary, avoid messy or strong-smelling food and dispose of waste responsibly.
  • Please keep the bus clean and dispose of waste responsibly.
4. Passenger Responsibilities
  • Keep your belongings secure. The operator, KPDL, and Life Republic Management are not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged items.
  • Passengers are responsible for getting off at the correct stop.
  • Inform the driver or conductor if you need assistance.
  • Report unattended bags or suspicious activity to the driver/conductor immediately.
5. Service Changes and Disruptions
  • Routes, timings, and halt locations may be changed as per management decisions when required.
  • Services may be delayed or temporarily suspended during adverse weather for safety reasons.
  • In case of a breakdown, replacement or alternate transport will be arranged as soon as possible.
  • Misconduct, harassment, or damage to bus property may result in removal from the service and legal action.

For further information, residents may contact the facility management team at 07030 111 137.

Happy Commuting!
