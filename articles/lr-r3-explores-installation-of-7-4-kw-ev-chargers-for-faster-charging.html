LR R3 Explores Installation of 7.4 kW EV Chargers for Faster Charging

By Community Desk  ·  27 Oct 2026 · 16:09

A proposed upgrade could reduce electric car charging time and improve convenience for residents.

LR R3 is considering the installation of 7.4 kW AC chargers to make electric vehicle (EV) charging faster and more convenient for residents. Concerned bodies are actively reviewing proposals for this upgrade, which could be a welcome step towards better EV charging facilities within the community.

Existing EV Charging Facilities

The society currently has six 3.3 kW EV chargers installed near the STP plant, opposite Building A. These chargers, provided by Greenie-Energy, have been working well, particularly for electric two-wheelers.

However, charging an electric car using these chargers can take up to 24 hours, depending on the vehicle's battery capacity, charging limits and the remaining battery level. This can be inconvenient for residents who rely on their electric cars for daily travel.

Proposed Upgrade to 7.4 kW Chargers

To address these concerns, the society is exploring the possibility of installing higher-capacity 7.4 kW AC chargers. The proposed upgrade aims to reduce the charging time for compatible electric cars to approximately 8–12 hours, depending on the vehicle and charging conditions.

If implemented, this facility could offer residents greater convenience and support the growing adoption of electric vehicles within the society.

The installation would also be a notable development for LR R3, potentially making it one of the first societies within Life Republic to introduce this charging facility.

A Positive Step Towards Sustainable Mobility

With electric vehicles becoming increasingly popular, accessible and efficient charging infrastructure is an important part of residential planning. The proposed upgrade reflects a positive approach towards addressing residents' needs and preparing the community for the future of mobility.

While the proposal is still under consideration, its implementation could benefit electric car owners and encourage more residents to consider EVs.

Residents will have to wait for official updates from the society regarding the final decision, installation timeline and operational details.

How to Use the Existing EV Chargers

Residents can currently use the six installed 3.3 kW Greenie-Energy chargers by following these steps:

  1. Locate the charging stations near the STP plant, opposite Building A.
  2. Download the Greenie Energie app on your smartphone.
  3. Sign up or register on the app.
  4. Scan the QR code displayed on the charging machine and follow the instructions to begin charging.

Current charging tariff: ₹13 per kWh, as per the information received.

Residents are encouraged to check the applicable tariff and charging instructions in the app before using the facility.
