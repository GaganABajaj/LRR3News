Society Management Issues Strict Warning Against Firecrackers Within Premises

By Community Desk  ·  24 Oct 2026 · 19:23

Residents warned of direct fines for violations; parents and flat owners to be held accountable

The Society Management has issued a strict and final warning prohibiting the bursting, lighting, or use of firecrackers anywhere within the society premises. The directive aims to prevent fire hazards, injuries, disturbances, and damage to society property.

According to the notice issued by the Society Management Office, any resident found violating the rule will be liable to a direct fine as prescribed under society rules. The penalty will be added directly to the maintenance bill of the respective flat.

The management has made it clear that no excuses or explanations will be entertained in cases of violation. The plea that a child or minor was responsible for bursting firecrackers will also not be accepted. Parents and flat owners will be held accountable for violations committed by their children, family members, guests, or visitors.

The notice further states that fines will be charged directly to the respective flat's maintenance account and requests for waivers will not be entertained.

Emphasising the seriousness of the matter, the Society Management has described the notice as a strict and final warning and urged all residents to comply with the prohibition without exception.

The management has appealed to residents to cooperate in maintaining a safe and peaceful environment within the society, warning that any violation will be dealt with strictly.

Original Society Notification

PROHIBITION OF FIRECRACKERS WITHIN SOCIETY PREMISES

Dear Residents,

This is to inform all residents that bursting, lighting, or using firecrackers anywhere within the society premises is strictly prohibited.

Any resident found violating this rule will be subject to a direct fine of (or the amount prescribed under society rules), which will be added directly to their flat's maintenance bill.

PLEASE NOTE THE FOLLOWING:
  • No excuses or explanations will be entertained.
  • The excuse that a child or minor was bursting firecrackers will not be accepted.
  • Parents and flat owners will be held responsible for violations by their children, family members, guests, or visitors.
  • The fine will be charged directly to the respective flat's maintenance account. No requests for waiver will be entertained.

This is a STRICT AND FINAL WARNING to all residents. Firecrackers create a serious risk of fire, injury, disturbance, and damage to society property.

All residents are instructed to comply with this rule without exception. Any violation will be dealt with strictly.

SOCIETY MANAGEMENT

Society Management Office
