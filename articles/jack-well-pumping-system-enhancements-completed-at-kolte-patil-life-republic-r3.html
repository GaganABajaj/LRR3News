Jack Well Pumping System Enhancements Completed at Kolte Patil Life Republic R3

By Community Desk  ·  22 Oct 2026 · 22:59

The Facility Management Team at Kolte Patil Life Republic has announced the successful completion of the Jack well pumping system enhancement, modification, and cleaning works. The team commenced the planned shutdown at 07:00 hrs and, despite challenging site conditions, managed to finish the critical interventions. The shutdown was extended by approximately four hours beyond the original schedule, but water supply from the WTP was started ahead of the planned shutdown time to reduce impact.

Following the extended shutdown, the water distribution schedule for the previous day was also prolonged to ensure all sectors received sufficient water. Over the next two days, the team will maintain the existing water distribution schedule, aiming for consistent and adequate supply across all sectors, subject to operational conditions and water availability.

The management expressed gratitude to Sector Office Bearers, Managing Committee Members, and residents for their support, cooperation, and patience during the extended shutdown. The enhancements are intended to improve the operational reliability of the Jack well pumping infrastructure and strengthen preparedness for ongoing water supply challenges.

Residents are urged to continue their cooperation as the team works to sustain reliable water services.
