Jackwell Pumping System Upgrade and Cleaning Completed at Life Republic

Update from Kolte-Patil Life Republic: The Facility Management Team has successfully completed the Jackwell pumping system enhancement and modification works, along with the cleaning of the Jackwell. The work aims to improve the reliability of the water infrastructure and strengthen the community’s preparedness to manage ongoing water supply challenges.

What Is a Jackwell?

A Jackwell is a water collection and pumping structure that receives water from a source, such as a river or reservoir, and helps pump it onward to a treatment plant or distribution system. At Life Republic, the Jackwell pumping infrastructure plays an important role in supporting the water supply process.

Extended Shutdown Due to Critical Work

According to the Facility Management Team, work commenced at 7:00 AM, with the team carrying out several critical interventions despite challenging site conditions. The shutdown extended approximately four hours beyond the planned duration due to the nature of the work and conditions at the site.

To help reduce the impact on residents, water supply from the Water Treatment Plant (WTP) was initiated ahead of the scheduled shutdown time. The team also made efforts to complete the work safely and minimize the duration of the interruption across all sectors.

Water Distribution: What Residents Can Expect

The extended shutdown also affected the previous day's water distribution schedule, pushing supply timings beyond their usual hours. However, the Facility Management Team stated that efforts were made to ensure all sectors received adequate water to meet their requirements.

Over the next two days, the team will endeavor to maintain the existing water distribution schedule and provide a consistent, adequate supply across all sectors, subject to operational conditions and water availability.

Management Thanks Residents for Their Cooperation

The Facility Management Team has expressed its appreciation to Sector Office Bearers, Managing Committee Members, and residents for their patience, understanding, and cooperation during the extended shutdown. The team acknowledged that this support helped it focus on completing the essential work successfully.

The completed enhancements and cleaning are expected to support the operational reliability of the Jackwell pumping infrastructure. Residents are encouraged to continue using water judiciously while the facility team works to maintain the regular distribution schedule.

Source: Facility Management Team, Kolte-Patil Life Republic
