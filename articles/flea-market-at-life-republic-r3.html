Flea Market at Life Republic R3

By Community Desk  ·  24 Oct 2026 · 12:04

A fun-filled evening awaits Life Republic R3 residents as a Flea Market is set to take place on Sunday, 25th October, from 4 PM to 10 PM.

The market will feature a variety of stalls offering trendy jewellery, clothing, packed food, live food counters and delicious desserts. Kids can also look forward to some bouncy fun, making it an enjoyable outing for the entire family.

Residents are invited to bring their family and friends to enjoy an evening of shopping, food and entertainment within the community.

Organised by: Bazaarly – Part of Deluxe Events
