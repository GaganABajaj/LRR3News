Water Supply Disruption Affects Flats in R3, Life Republic Township

By Community Desk  ·  30 Oct 2026 · 12:35

Residents of R3 in Life Republic Township are experiencing a temporary disruption in their water supply due to a delay in the regular water delivery from the township. The delay has affected the availability of water in residential flats, causing inconvenience to residents.

The R3 Manager has been in constant communication with the township management regarding the issue. Efforts are being made to restore the water supply; however, residents have been informed that it may take some time before water reaches all buildings and normal supply is resumed.

Understanding How Water Reaches Your Flat

To help residents understand the situation, here is a brief explanation of how the water distribution system works within R3.

The township management/builder is responsible for supplying clean water to all sectors of Life Republic. The water is generally supplied once a day, with each sector receiving a designated quantity based on the number of residential flats.

R3 receives its allocated water supply through this arrangement, following a regular daily schedule.

The distribution process within R3 works as follows:

  1. Water supply from the township: Water is first delivered by the township to the common storage tank in R3.
  2. Initial tank filling: Once the common storage tank reaches approximately 50% capacity, water distribution to the individual buildings begins. Reaching this initial level generally takes around 1.5 hours.
  3. Distribution to building tanks: Water from the common storage tank is diverted to the overhead tanks of different buildings.
  4. Building-level distribution: Each building has two overhead water tanks, which serve different sections of the building. Each tank supplies approximately half of the flats.
  5. Flushing water storage: Buildings also have separate flushing water tanks located around the middle floors to support the flushing water requirements.

This entire process takes time, which is why water may not reach every flat immediately after the township resumes its supply.

Expected Water Supply Timeline

Due to the delay in the township's regular water delivery, the expected timeline for restoring water availability in R3 is as follows:

Activity Expected Time Water supply received from township 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM Common storage tank reaches 50% capacity 2:30 PM – 3:30 PM Water supply to buildings begins gradually Around 3:30 PM Building overhead tanks expected to be completely filled 5:30 PM – 6:00 PM

Note: These timings are estimates and may vary depending on the actual water delivery time and the rate at which the storage tanks are filled.

A Request to Residents

Residents are requested to remain patient and cooperate while the water supply is being restored. Since water distribution takes place in stages, some buildings and flats may receive water earlier than others.

Meanwhile, residents are encouraged to use the available water carefully and avoid unnecessary consumption until the regular supply is fully restored.

The R3 Management is following up with the township management to ensure that the water supply reaches the sector as soon as possible.

We appreciate everyone's patience and cooperation during this temporary inconvenience.
