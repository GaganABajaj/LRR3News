Water Shortage Delays Society Tank Cleaning

By Community Desk  ·  27 Oct 2026 · 15:30

The cleaning of water tanks within the society has been postponed indefinitely due to a shortage of water.

The cleaning work, which was scheduled for the third and fourth weeks of October 2026, will not take place as planned.

Although there are informal suggestions that the delay may extend beyond Diwali, this has not been officially confirmed. A revised schedule will be announced later, depending on water availability.

The situation is a matter of concern, as the monsoon season has only recently ended, yet the society is already facing a water shortage. This highlights the importance of careful water management and responsible consumption, especially during periods of limited supply.

Residents are requested to use water judiciously, avoid unnecessary wastage, and cooperate with the society to help manage the available resources until the situation improves.

Further updates regarding the tank cleaning schedule will be shared once more information becomes available.
