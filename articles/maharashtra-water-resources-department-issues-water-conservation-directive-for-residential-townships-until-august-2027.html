Maharashtra Water Resources Department Issues Water Conservation Directive for Residential Townships Until August 2027

By Community Desk  ·  23 Oct 2026 · 12:29

The Water Resources Department of the Government of Maharashtra, via the Lift Irrigation Sub‑Division No. 4 in Tathawade, has issued an official communication warning of potential water scarcity caused by the El Niño phenomenon. The department has instructed all residential townships and related establishments to adopt systematic water‑management measures to ensure a reliable supply of drinking water through 31 August 2027.

Key actions mandated by the department include:

1. **Minimum 10% reduction in water consumption** – Facilities must review daily demand, identify optimisation opportunities, and achieve at least a ten‑percent cut in overall usage. 2. **Leakage prevention and wastage control** – Conduct thorough inspections of water supply lines, internal distribution networks, pipelines, and storage tanks to promptly repair any leaks. 3. **Control of overflow and unnecessary usage** – Monitor storage tanks and distribution systems to avoid overflow, unnecessary discharge, and avoidable wastage. 4. **Optimisation of water usage** – Promote responsible consumption across residential units, common areas, amenities, and operational facilities. 5. **Monitoring and compliance** – Keep regular records of water consumption, assess the effectiveness of conservation measures, and ensure adherence to the department’s directives.

The Facilities Management Team has called on department heads and relevant teams to cooperate fully and treat water conservation as a shared responsibility. Implementation of these measures is to be coordinated and time‑bound to mitigate the risk of water scarcity in the coming months.

The official communication from the Irrigation Department has been attached for reference.
