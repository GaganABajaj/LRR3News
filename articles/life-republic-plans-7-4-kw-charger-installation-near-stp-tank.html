Life Republic Plans 7.4 kW Charger Installation Near STP Tank

By Community Desk  ·  22 Oct 2026 · 23:08

Life Republic has announced that a new 7.4 kW charger will be installed near the STP Tank. The installation is intended to provide additional charging capacity for electric vehicles in the vicinity of the STP Tank. No further details about the timeline or operational aspects were provided.
