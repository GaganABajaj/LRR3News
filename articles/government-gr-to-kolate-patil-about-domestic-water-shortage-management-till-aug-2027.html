Government GR to Kolate Patil about Domestic Water Shortage Management Till Aug 2027

By Community Desk  ·  24 Oct 2026 · 12:16

Government Directs Kolte-Patil to Implement Water Conservation Measures Until August 2027

In view of the anticipated impact of the El Niño phenomenon on rainfall and the growing risk of water scarcity across Maharashtra, the Government of Maharashtra has directed establishments, including residential townships, to adopt systematic water conservation measures to safeguard drinking water availability until August 31, 2027.

According to a communication shared by the Facilities Management Team, Kolte-Patil has received an official notification from the Water Resources Department through the Lift Irrigation Sub-Division No. 4, Tathawade. The directive calls for a minimum 10% reduction in overall water consumption, along with measures to prevent leakage, control wastage and improve water management.

What Measures Are Being Planned?

The communication calls upon the concerned departments and teams to implement the following measures on priority:

  • Reduce water consumption: Review daily water demand and consumption, identify opportunities for optimization, and achieve at least a 10% reduction.
  • Prevent leaks: Inspect pipelines, internal distribution networks and storage tanks, and promptly repair any leakages.
  • Control overflow: Monitor water storage tanks and distribution systems to prevent overflow and unnecessary discharge.
  • Promote responsible usage: Optimize water consumption across residential areas, common spaces, amenities and operational facilities.
  • Monitor consumption: Maintain regular water consumption records and assess the effectiveness of conservation measures to ensure compliance.

Why This Matters to Residents

With water availability potentially under pressure in the coming months, proactive conservation will be important for maintaining a reliable supply across the township. Preventing wastage, reporting leaks promptly and using water responsibly can help reduce overall demand and support the community's long-term water security.

While the communication primarily directs the facilities management and concerned operational teams to implement these measures, residents can also contribute by avoiding unnecessary water usage and promptly reporting overflowing tanks or visible leakages to the management team.

A Shared Responsibility

The Facilities Management Team has urged all departments and concerned teams to cooperate in implementing the measures in a coordinated and time-bound manner. The official communication from the Irrigation Department has been shared for reference and necessary action.

The message is clear: conserving water today is essential to protecting its availability tomorrow. Collective efforts by the management and residents can help Life Republic navigate potential water shortages while ensuring responsible use of this vital resource.

Source: Communication issued by the Facilities Management Team. The government directive is referenced in the communication shared by the management.
