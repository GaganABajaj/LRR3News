Know Your Neighbour – A New Initiative on LRR3News.in!

By Community Desk  ·  06 Nov 2026 · 22:52

LRR3News.in is launching a new community series called Know Your Neighbour. The initiative begins on 8 November 2026 and aims to feature a different resident from Life Republic, R3 Sector, every Sunday.

Each feature will be a light-hearted, informal interview conducted either face-to-face or remotely via email or chat, depending on the participant’s preference. The goal is to help residents discover common interests, share experiences, and potentially make new friends.

Anyone with an interesting hobby, unique experience, passion, or story is invited to take part. Submissions are welcome from anyone who wishes to introduce themselves to their neighbours.

You can browse the interviews in our Know Your Neighbour series.

Would you like to be featured? Simply drop us an email at nextdoor@lrr3news.in, and we will get in touch to take the conversation forward.

Let's get to know each other better and make Life Republic a more connected community, one neighbour at a time!

Stay tuned for the first conversation on LRR3News.in and look out for new interviews every Sunday.
