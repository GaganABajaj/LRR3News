Advertise with Life Republic R3 News

Promote your business, services, products, or activities to the Life Republic R3 community.

🎉 Advertisements For LR-R3 Are FREE Forever!

Advertisements for Life Republic R3 residents, owners, and flats will be published free of charge forever.

How to Request an Advertisement

Follow these simple steps to submit your advertisement for publication on the Life Republic R3 News website.

  1. Prepare an email addressed to grow@lrr3news.in .
  2. Provide the advertisement details using the format given below.
  3. Attach the advertisement image that you want displayed on the website.
  4. Include your contact or social media links, if applicable, so interested residents can reach you.
  5. Click the Draft Advertisement Email button below to open a pre-filled email. Review the information, attach your image, and send it.

What Will Your Advertisement Look Like?

Your advertisement will be displayed as a card on the Life Republic R3 News website. The card may include your advertisement image, title, description, advertiser name, category, badge, and relevant links.

Information Required

Please include the following details in your email:

1. Advertisement Name
The title or name of your advertisement.

2. Advertisement Image
Attach the image you want displayed on your advertisement card.

3. Details
A brief description of your product, service, business, or activity.

4. Advertiser Name
Your name or business name.

5. Badge (if applicable)
Specify a short label, if required, such as "Resident-Owned", "Special Offer", or "New".

6. Facebook / Instagram / Other Page Link
Provide the URL of your business page, social media profile, or website, if available.

7. Category
Specify the relevant category, such as Food, Education, Home Services, Health & Wellness, Shopping, Professional Services, or Other.

✉ Draft Advertisement Email

Clicking this button opens your email application with the recipient, subject, and message template filled in. Please attach your advertisement image before sending.
